A boy moved into a tiny apartment above an old convenience store in a city where he knew nobody. Every night at exactly 11:47 PM, he heard someone playing piano from the building next door. The melody was always the same. Soft, unfinished, almost like the song was waiting for something.

One rainy evening, the music suddenly stopped halfway through.

Curious, he grabbed his jacket and followed the soundless silence down the alley between the buildings. At the back door of the neighboring apartment, he found a small note taped to the handle:

"If you know how the song ends, please finish it for me."

He slowly opened the door and stepped inside… **Reader has to complete the story.**
